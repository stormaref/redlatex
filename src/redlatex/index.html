What it catches

redlatex compares what a reader sees, the compiled pages, not the LaTeX source. These are real crops from its output.

Every changed word, in place

Removed words are highlighted in red on the earlier page and added words in green on the revised page, with a bar in the margin beside each changed line.

Changed figures, pixel by pixel

Plots, diagrams and images are compared as rendered pixels. A figure that changed gets a dashed frame on both sides, even when it has no text.

Moved text is not an edit

When LaTeX moves a table or figure, or you move a paragraph, only what actually changed is marked. Here, just the new numbers.

A summary per section

The first page counts removed and added words for every section, with links to the right page, and the PDF has a bookmark per section.
